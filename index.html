Scouting summary

Player type

Left-sided creative attacker who is comfortable on either wing, in the number 10 role or at left-back. Two-footed, so he can cut inside or go outside. Gets his threat from dribbling and 1v1 situations, sees passes early and presses to win the ball high. At 163 cm, he relies on quickness and technique rather than size, and is working on strength and stamina.
